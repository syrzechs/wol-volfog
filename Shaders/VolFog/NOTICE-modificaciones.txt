2026-10-19  Brief B2 (World of Legends, native volumetric fog, phase 3).
  - vf_common.hlsli, vf_integrate.hlsli, vf_local_lights.hlsli, vf_composite.hlsli, vf_sample_split.hlsli,
    vf_march.hlsl, vf_temporal.hlsl, vf_history_depth.hlsl, vf_composite.hlsl, vf_silhouette_mask.hlsl,
    vf_ray_mask.hlsl, vf_ray_blur.hlsl, vf_ray_composite.hlsl, vf_density_variation.hlsli:
    copied byte for byte, unchanged. They are not compiled yet (briefs B6 and later). Planned changes, not applied
    here: kSkyMinDepth 0.99903 -> 0.95 in vf_common.hlsli and tex3D -> 2D atlas in vf_density_variation.hlsli.
  - vf_fullscreen_vs.hlsl: copied byte for byte and compiled as it is (vs_3_0, WolVolFogQuad.bls). It returns its POSITION
    input untouched, so the adaptation to the client is on the CPU side: VolFog::Render draws the FFX quad
    (FFX::SetupVB_PCT, vertex format position + colour + texcoord) with its positions already in clip space (-1..1).
    The colour and texcoord attributes are present in the vertex buffer and ignored by the shader.
  - WolVolFogDebug.hlsl: new file written for this project (not derived from CoAVolFog code), same licence.
  - Build: the shaders are compiled with fxc (Windows SDK) from CMake, packed in the GXSH ".bls" container by
    Tools/VolFog/mkbls.py and stored in bin/Win/Data/patch-Z.MPQ by Tools/VolFog/mkmpq.py.

2026-10-20  Brief B6 (World of Legends, native volumetric fog, phase 3: march and composite).
  - vf_common.hlsli: kSkyMinDepth 0.99903 -> 0.95 (the only change; the client draws the world in the viewport depth range 0..0.94 and the sky
    in 0.999..1.0 without writing depth, so depth >= 0.95 is always sky). This was one of the two planned changes of B2.
  - vf_density_variation.hlsli: tex3D -> 2D atlas (the other planned change). The Gx layer has no volume textures, so the 32^3 density noise is a
    2D atlas of 32 slices of 32x32 texels stacked along v (32 x 1024, A8R8G8B8 grey, LINEAR, WRAP in u); sampler3D sDensityNoise (s9) becomes
    sampler2D, each tex3Dlod becomes SampleNoiseVolume(): two tex2Dlod of the slices that bracket z plus a lerp. v stays inside its slice, so
    the y wrap of the volume is a hard step instead of a blend, once every 32 texels. DensityVariation() keeps its signature and its constants.
  - vf_authored_noise.hlsli: copied byte for byte from 205ade5 (vf_integrate.hlsli includes it). It is compiled with SAMPLES_AUTHORED_NOISE = 0
    (its default), so none of its code or registers (c36.., s10) reaches the pixel shaders; the authored noise of the Classic layers is not used.
  - vf_march.hlsl and vf_composite.hlsl are compiled (CMakeLists.txt, six permutations each: STEPS 16 / 24 / 32 x LOCAL_LIGHTS 0 / 1, the macros of
    vf_integrate.hlsli); the sources are unchanged.
  - Engine/Source/VolFog/VolFogNoise.cpp: new, DensityNoiseValue derived from CreateDensityNoise of src/noise_volume.cpp (same hash; the volume is
    uploaded as the atlas above through GxTexCreate).
  - Engine/Source/VolFog/VolFogRender.cpp: the packing of the constants (c0-c13 common block, layers at c12, c53, c78, c96-c98) and the order of the
    passes follow Renderer::Draw of src/renderer.cpp, rewritten over Gx (render targets, texture units, shader constants). Left out for now: the
    temporal filter (B7), the local lights (B8) and the light shafts (B9).

2026-10-20  Brief B9 (World of Legends, native volumetric fog, phase 3: light shafts and NaN guard).
  - vf_ray_mask.hlsl, vf_ray_blur.hlsl and vf_ray_composite.hlsl are compiled as they are (CMakeLists.txt, one permutation each: WolVolFogRayMask,
    WolVolFogRayBlur, WolVolFogRayComposite); no change in the three sources. Registers as in coa-vfog: mask c9 cRayMask (view direction to the body
    + falloff exponent 8), c10 cRaySource (0, luminance threshold 0.12, 1 / ray width, 1 / ray height), s0 depth, s1 reduced scene; blur c9
    cRadialBlur (body uv, step fraction, 1 / sum of the tap weights), c10 cRayTarget (ray size, texel); blend c96-c98 cComposite (y = strength),
    cGodRayColour, cSun (xy = body in pixels), s2 shafts, s3 the image to blend over.
  - NaN guard. vf_temporal.hlsl: main() is now a wrapper that sends the filter result (TemporalResult(), the old main) through SanitiseHistory():
    radiance clamped to 0..1024 with a comparison (a NaN fails "x >= 0" and becomes 0, because max(NaN, 0) is not defined the same way on every
    driver) and the fourth channel saturated; the 16 bit history is read back by the next frame, so a NaN or an infinity written once would stay for
    good (the B8 report mentions one unexplained dark band; this closes the way for non finite values to persist). vf_composite.hlsli: SanitiseFog()
    on the fog read back from the march / history right after the exposure (radiance 0..1024, opacity saturated), and SanitiseGodRays() (0..16) in
    BlendGodRays() in place of max(godRays, 0). Before this brief neither shader bounded its output: BlendGodRays only had max(godRays, 0), and the
    temporal filter had no guard at all (its clamp() against the neighbourhood range lets a NaN through). Both lines are the only changes in the two files.
  - WolVolFogDebug.hlsl (new in B2, ours): permutation 4 (DEBUG_MODE=4, wolVolFogDebug 6) shows the shaft texture (s1) over the world viewport, scaled
    by c4.x (0 = black, used when the frame draws no shafts).
  - Engine/Source/VolFog/VolFogRender.cpp: the targets (rays[0], rays[1], ARGB8, LINEAR, a quarter of the world viewport), the reduced scene copy
    (GxTexStretch = StretchRect with a linear filter, CopySceneForGodRays), the mask, the two radial blurs (step 0.075 and 0.075 / 8, 8 taps, decay 0.9,
    DrawGodRayMask) and the blend (DrawGodRaysOverScene, over a second copy of the fogged image: the late path of coa-vfog, not inside the composition
    shader) follow renderer.cpp, rewritten over Gx. The silhouette mask (vf_silhouette_mask.hlsl, MarkSilhouetteSamples) is not part of the shafts in coa-vfog:
    it only serves the sample-split composite of multisampled depth (DrawCompositeBySampleDepth), so it stays unused here (the fog forces MSAA off).
  - Engine/Source/VolFog/VolFogRays.cpp (new): ComputeRayFrame(), the sun block of Renderer::RenderPasses (position of the body on the screen from the
    view and projection of the frame, screen fade clamp(1.6 - max(|ndc|), 0, 1), strength). Added: the strength is also multiplied by the occlusion
    visibility of the sun or moon (FrameInputs::rayVisibility, filled in WoW/Source/WorldClient/VolFogWorld.cpp from DNGlare::GetQueryOcclusion, or
    GetPointOcclusion without a query, eased up 4 / s and down 1.5 / s). DNGlare::m_screenGlow is not used: the fade of the moon glare is 0 under a
    clear sky (DNMoonGlare::GetCloudDensityFade), when the moon is most visible.
  - wolVolFogDump (VolFogDump.cpp) prints two "rays:" lines; wolVolFogDebug 6 is accepted (WorldParam_CVar.cpp) and keeps the fog armed (VolFog.cpp).

2026-10-20  Brief B10 (World of Legends, native volumetric fog, phase 3: interiors, water, glow and the corrections of the B7-B8 review).
  - Engine/Source/VolFog/VolFogLights.h / VolFogLights.cpp (derived from src/engine_lights.* of 205ade5): the cut-off of PointLightCutoff is 1/32 of the
    peak colour instead of 1/256 (coa-vfog's value), so the sphere of a light of the client (fixed attenuation 0; 0.7; 0.03) goes from 81 to 23 yards; lights
    whose faded colour is below the cut-off are not uploaded. Not in coa-vfog: the lights are only scattered from wolVolFog 2 on.
  - Engine/Source/VolFog/VolFogModel.h / VolFogModel.cpp (derived from src/fog_model.*): the moon fades in as the sun sets (visibility multiplied by
    1 - SmoothStep(-0.15, -0.03, sun elevation)); the body is the moon only when the sun is below -0.03 (WoW/Source/WorldClient/VolFogWorld.cpp). coa-vfog
    switches at the horizon and lets the light jump. No other change in the model: the interior weights (15 % of the scene layers, 100 % of the distance
    layer, no sun) and the distance layer of fogMode 1 maps were measured and kept as they are.
  - Engine/Source/VolFog/VolFogRender.cpp: s9 (the density noise atlas) is bound in the composition as coa-vfog does all frame; cSun.w / the glow
    compensation constant of both composition shaders is the glow of the client (FrameInputs::clientGlowAmount, 0 unless ffxGlow is on and the glow is
    the active screen effect), the GlowCompensation of coa-vfog (renderer.cpp:1374, 1496); the temporal history is dropped when the lit / unlit march
    variant changes or the light texture is made anew. No shader source changed.

2026-10-20  Brief B13 (World of Legends, native volumetric fog, phase 3: MSAA with the readable depth).
  - Engine/Source/VolFog/VolFogResz.cpp (new, derived from src/msaa_depth.cpp, src/fullscreen_triangle.h, shaders/vf_fullscreen_vs.hlsl and
    shaders/vf_depth_check.hlsl of 205ade5): the RESZ resolve (ResolveThroughResz: INTZ in stage 0, a dummy point with Z and colour writes off, the writes
    back on, D3DRS_POINTSIZE = 0x7FA05000) and the self-test (two known depths written to the multisampled depth, resolved, read back through a small
    R32F target, twice with the halves swapped). Changes: the device state is saved and restored with a D3DSBT_ALL state block (the client creates a pure
    device; coa-vfog's per-state Get* lists cannot work there), the two tiny shaders are embedded as vs_2_0 / ps_2_0 tokens (fxc output of the same two
    HLSL files with tex2D, no build step), the small target is unbound before GetRenderTargetData, and the self-test and the per-frame resolve share one
    code path. The NvAPI path of msaa_depth.cpp is not ported (NVIDIA's SDK is not downloaded); the hook point is documented in VolFogDepth.cpp (WOL_NVAPI).
  - Engine/Source/VolFog/VolFogDepth.cpp: the "MSAA + copy" mode (INTZ is a copy of the multisampled AutoDepthStencil, refreshed by ResolveDepth() at the
    start of VolFog::Render()); a D24X8 request becomes D24S8 for the multisampled depth, as coa-vfog does. Not in coa-vfog: the copy mode is refused when
    d3d9.dll is the system (native) copy, because the RESZ trigger hangs the GPU of the AMD RX 9070 XT there (see the comment in the file).
